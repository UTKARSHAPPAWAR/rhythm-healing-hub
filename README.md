MASTER PROMPT

Build a one-page, mobile-responsive website for Rhythm Healing, a holistic energy-healing and wellness practice run by Dr. Vaishali. The tagline is "Raise Yourself to Help Mankind."

Overall vibe: calm, elegant, healing, minimalistic, trustworthy — like a boutique wellness studio, not a clinical medical site. Generous white space, soft rounded corners, gentle shadows, smooth scroll-triggered fade-in animations. Nothing loud or corporate.

Design System

Color palette: soft pastels — lavender (#D8C7E8 or similar), mint green (#C8E6D9), warm beige (#F3ECE1), white (#FFFFFF), with a deeper plum or sage as an accent for buttons/headings (e.g. #6B4E71 or #5C7A63).

Typography: a modern, clean serif or soft sans-serif for headings (e.g. "Cormorant" or "Playfair Display" for elegance) paired with a highly readable sans-serif for body text (e.g. "Inter" or "Poppins"). Generous line-height, not too bold.

Layout: spacious sections, centered content with max-width containers, soft dividers between sections (e.g. subtle wave or gradient transitions).

Imagery: nature-inspired — soft gradients, calming visuals (mountains, water, light, lotus/mandala motifs), no stock-photo clichés of stressed people. Use icons (from a set like Lucide or Phosphor icons) in a consistent line-style for services and highlights.

Performance: fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop), fast-loading, compressed images, SEO-friendly meta tags and semantic HTML structure.

Build these sections in order:

1. Hero Section

Rhythm Healing logo placeholder (top left) and site name

Large, elegant headline: tagline "Raise Yourself to Help Mankind"

Short supporting line: one sentence about helping people raise their inner vibration and achieve holistic well-being

Soft nature-inspired background (gradient in the pastel palette, or a subtle abstract image)

A prominent "Contact / Book a Session" button that scrolls to the Contact section

Sticky/simple nav bar with links to About, Services, Why Us, Appointments, Contact

2. About Rhythm Healing

Heading: "About Rhythm Healing"

2–3 short paragraphs covering: the vision and philosophy of Rhythm Healing, how energy healing supports emotional and spiritual growth, and the personalized, compassionate approach taken with every client

Optional: a warm, calming photo or illustration alongside the text (image on one side, text on the other on desktop; stacked on mobile)

3. Healing Services

Heading: "Our Healing Services"

A responsive grid of 6 cards (3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile), each with an icon, service name, a short description, key benefits, and who it's suitable for. Use these six services:

Healing Modality Combination – Integrative approach combining multiple healing techniques for deeper transformation

Private Counselling – One-on-one sessions for emotional clarity and personal growth

Family Constellation Therapy – Healing family patterns and restoring harmony in relationships

EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique) – Releasing emotional blockages through tapping therapy

Theta Healing – Accessing subconscious beliefs to create positive life changes

Inner Child Healing – Reconnecting with and nurturing the inner child for emotional balance

Cards should have a soft hover effect (lift + shadow) and consistent icon style

4. Why Choose Rhythm Healing

Heading: "Why Choose Rhythm Healing"

A 3x2 (or 2x3 on mobile) grid of six highlights, each with a simple icon and short label:

Personalized Healing

Confidential Sessions

Holistic Wellness

Emotional Balance

Inner Transformation

Weekend Appointment Availability

5. Appointment Information

Heading: "Book Your Session"

Clearly state: available Saturday & Sunday, plus weekday evenings

Mention limited slots to ensure focused, quality sessions

A call-to-action button linking to WhatsApp or the Contact section

6. Contact Section

Heading: "Get in Touch"

Display: Mobile number, WhatsApp (clickable to open chat), Email (clickable mailto), Location/address

Use icon + label layout for each contact method, in a clean card or list format

Optional simple contact form (Name, Email, Message) if easy to include, but the core requirement is the direct contact details above

7. Footer

Copyright line: "© [current year] Rhythm Healing. All rights reserved."

Social media icon links: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn (use placeholder URLs I can update later)

Repeat key contact details (phone/email) for easy access

Technical requirements

Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints

Smooth scrolling navigation between sections

Fast-loading, optimized images

Clean, semantic, accessible HTML (proper heading hierarchy, alt text on images, ARIA labels where relevant)

SEO-friendly: page title, meta description, and structured headings reflecting "Rhythm Healing," "energy healing," "holistic wellness," "Dr. Vaishali"

Subtle scroll-based fade-in/slide-up animations on each section (not overdone)
